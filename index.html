System Manual & How to Navigate

🌐 HOW TO NAVIGATE THE WEB APP

1. Importing Your Build from PC (forge_master_build.json)

You can sync your existing PC character build to your phone in three simple steps:

  1. Click the 💾 JSON button in the top navigation bar.
  2. Click 📂 Choose .json File and select your forge_master_build.json, OR paste its entire text into the text area.
  3. Click 📥 Import JSON. All 12 gear slots, Mount & Pet storages, and Fairy levels will immediately load and recalculate!
  4. Legacy Conversion: Older build files without the x1000 multiplier are automatically upgraded to full true values on import.
  5. Auto-Save: All changes are continuously saved to your browser's local memory (localStorage), preserving your build even when you close the tab.
2. Equipment Calibration & Rarity (Slots 1–8)
  • Click any gear slot (1–8) to open its calibration window.
  • Rarity Tiers: Assign an Era/Rarity tier (Primitive, Medieval, Early-Modern, Modern, Space, Interstellar, Multiverse, Quantum, Underworld, Divine) to activate glowing border highlights and badges.
  • Slot 6 (Dedicated Weapon): Toggle between ⚔️ MELEE [M] and 🏹 RANGED [R]. Wrong weapon types trigger a hardware-level cutoff of incompatible stats.
  • True Metric Inputs: Flat HP and Flat DMG input fields support raw numbers and metric suffixes (e.g. 20k = 20,000, 1.3m = 1,300,000).
  • Marginal Simulator: Tweak stats and click 🔍 Simulate Swap to test drops, compare Real DPS and HPS, and evaluate Item Quality Score % without modifying your live build. Click 💾 Save to Build to equip.
3. Storage Optimizer (Mount & Pets)
  • Click ⚡ STORAGE OPTIMIZER in the Mount & Pets section.
  • Add inventory mounts and pets using + Add Mount / + Add Pet.
  • Click ⚡ RUN OPTIMIZER: Evaluates all mathematical combinations of 1 Mount and 3 Pets against your current armor, enforcing the 30% Lifesteal survival protocol while maximizing all-around raw DPS.
  • Click Apply to Build to immediately equip the winning setup.

⚙️ BINARY MECHANICS & BREAKPOINTS

Attack Speed Breakpoints (10 Ticks / sec)

The game animation engine runs at rigid 0.1s tick intervals. Attack speed does NOT scale continuously; it snaps to rigid breakpoints:

+11.2% -> 1.0s | +25.1% -> 0.9s | +42.9% -> 0.8s | +66.7% -> 0.7s
+100.1% -> 0.6s | +150.1% -> 0.5s | +203.0% -> 0.4s | +233.4% -> 0.35s | +400.1% -> 0.3s

Any Attack Speed above 150.1% that fails to hit the next tier is penalized in quality scoring as wasted stats.

Fairy Mira (Crit Fairy)

Every 15% Skill Damage (SD) grants 1 Fairy Block. Each block converts to Crit Chance based on your Fairy Level (capped at 80% bonus CC).

Survival Protocol (Lifesteal)

A minimum of 30% Lifesteal (LS) is strictly required by the simulator to ensure late-game dungeon survival.
