System Information & Mechanics

Attack Speed Breakpoints

Attack Speed does NOT scale smoothly. It snaps to rigid animation intervals:

+11.2% -> 1.0s | +25.1% -> 0.9s | +42.9% -> 0.8s
+66.7% -> 0.7s | +100.1% -> 0.6s | +150.1% -> 0.5s
+203.0% -> 0.4s | +233.4% -> 0.35s | +400.1% -> 0.3s

Equipment Rarities (Slots 1-8)

Primitive (Szary), Medieval (Niebieski), Early-Modern (Zielony), Modern (Żółty), Space (Czerwony), Interstellar (Jasny fioletowy), Multiverse (Błękitny), Quantum (Ciemny fioletowy), Underworld (Brązowy), Divine (Pomarańczowy).

Metric Inputs (k & m)

Base Flat HP and Flat DMG input fields support raw true numbers and metric abbreviations (e.g. 20k = 20,000, 1.3m = 1,300,000).

Fairy Mira (Crit Fairy)

Every 15% Skill Damage (SD) grants 1 Fairy Block. Each block converts to Crit Chance based on your Fairy Level (capped at 80% bonus CC).
